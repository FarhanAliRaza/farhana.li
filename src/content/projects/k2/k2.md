K2 keeps the part of Alpine.js people like, declarative `x-data`, `x-text`, `x-show`, `x-model`, and `@event` directives in plain HTML, and rebuilds the reactivity on the TC39 Signals proposal.

- **Tiny.** 3.6KB gzipped, compared with roughly 14KB for Alpine.js and 7KB for Petite-Vue.
- **Fast.** Signals give fine-grained updates, so a change re-renders only the bindings that depend on it.
- **Familiar.** Existing Alpine.js markup mostly works as-is.
- **Zero dependencies**, written in TypeScript, published on npm as `@farhanaliraza/k2-js`.
